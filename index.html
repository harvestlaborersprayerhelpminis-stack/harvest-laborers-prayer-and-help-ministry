1. Home (Ministry کا تعارف)

Welcome to Harvest Laborers Prayer and Help Ministry.

Our Main Goal: Our service is not limited to physical help, but to the preaching of the Gospel of Christ, love, freedom, and hope.

“Therefore go and make disciples of all nations...” (Matthew 28:19–20)

2. About Us (Vision اور Mission)

Vision: To transform lives through spiritual guidance, prayer, and practical help to vulnerable communities.

Mission: Reaching out to children, widows, and families in need with spiritual support, education, and relief assistance.

3. Statement of Faith

We believe in the power of God, divine love, prayer, and living in fellowship to serve humanity according to Biblical principles.

4. In What Areas Will We Work?

1. Food Support

Supporting families deprived of daily bread through ration distribution (flour, rice, lentils, oil). Special help for widows and orphans.

“He who has a good eye will be blessed...” (Proverbs 22:9)

2. Children’s Education

Providing Christian education so no child's future is stolen by poverty.

“Train up a child in the way he should go...” (Proverbs 22:6)

3. Help for the Poor and Helpless

Standing with widows, orphans, and disabled individuals.

“Do justice to the poor and orphans...” (Psalm 82:3)

4. Help for Prisoners and the Oppressed

Supporting those falsely prosecuted, burdened by debt, or oppressed.

“To proclaim liberty to the captives...” (Isaiah 61:1; Luke 4:18)

5. Dowry Support

Helping poor girls with essential household goods so they can begin married life with dignity.

“God sets the lonely in families...” (Psalm 68:6)

6. Above All: Preaching the Word of God

Every act of help includes prayer and sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ everywhere.
